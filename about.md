      <h2>A bit about me</h2>
      <p>My work has taken me through application development, backend engineering, and developer productivity. I currently work at Salesforce on engineering insights and developer productivity. Before that, I worked on engineering platforms and developer experience at Informatica.</p>
      <p>I'm curious about how reliable software gets built: the architecture underneath it, the tools that help teams do their best work, and the small decisions that make systems easier to understand and maintain. Lately, I've also been exploring how AI is changing the way software is designed and delivered.</p>
      <p>Penguin Coders is my personal engineering publication—a home for notes, experiments, and ideas I want to understand more deeply.</p>
    </section>
